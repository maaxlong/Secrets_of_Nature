# Secrets_of_Nature
Data relating to the 1920s-1940s series of short natural history films "Secrets of Nature" and "Secrets of Life"

Data is available for CSV download, and also viewable online via [Airtable](https://airtable.com/app9zI4ydxYZTSixu/shr3fGVDr9QZAvHMs). 

# Map
Data from the sample map of Secrets of Nature screenings published in _A Science for Everyday Life_(2026) available as a CSV: [secrets-map-data.csv](https://github.com/maaxlong/Secrets_of_Nature/blob/main/secrets-map-data.csv). This shows the locations of 50 screenings of Secrets of Nature films across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland between 1923 and 1939, along with source information for each screening. The majority of these screenings were identified from classified advertisements and other articles from digitised newspapers in the British Newspaper Archive.
