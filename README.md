# Secrets_of_Nature
Data relating to the 1920s-1940s series of short natural history films "Secrets of Nature" and "Secrets of Life"

Data is available for CSV download, and also viewable online via [Airtable](https://airtable.com/app9zI4ydxYZTSixu/shr3fGVDr9QZAvHMs). 

# Map
Data from the sample map of Secrets of Nature screenings published in _A Science for Everyday Life_(2026) available as a CSV: [secrets-map-data.csv](https://github.com/maaxlong/Secrets_of_Nature/blob/main/secrets-map-data.csv)
